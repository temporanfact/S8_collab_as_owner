The Axialla
>Axial'law
>>>
  The Axial'law (A.K.A the Axialla) is the Law of Axes and Axioms. It is the backbone of all reality. 
  Phrased in an actually meaningful way: It is space. It defines size and location for anything and everything that exists. It defines the axes of the world. It is also able to contain "fundaments" that expand the laws. 
  The fundaments are fully separate from the Axialla and operate in tandem rather than sequentially. This order of operations makes it possible for the Axialla and one of the fundaments to temporarily swap roles. This is what happens at the singularity of a blackhole, where time and space have their roles swapped.
>>>

>Fundaments
>>Seios-Aukh
>>>
  Seios-Aukh was the first fundament to be integrated into the Axialla, and some argue it is the most important. Seios-Aukh is time. It grants motion and causal interactions to all things that exist in this reality. Seios-Aukh is electromagnetic radiation, spatial distortion (e.g. gravity), the electromagnetic force, and kinetic energy. Without Seios-Aukh, nothing could DO anything.
  Seios-Aukh is believed by some to have come from the Aukh’star, a theoretical convergence of limitless information whose existence was proposed by a scientist from the earliest days of the UCC’s reign. In the centuries since, sparse evidence has been found to prove the existence of the Aukh’star. It is widely regarded as myth, yet still many (particularly citizens of certain religious faiths) believe the Aukh’star to exist; some even believe it to be an omnipotent God.
>>>

>>Uithyl-Kaust and Umrik-Kaust’bringer
>>>
  Uithyl-Kaust is the secondary basis of reality. It is mass and matter (two separate things in this world). It is believed to originate from an ancient entity called Umrik-Kaust’bringer. There is ample evidence to prove this entity’s existence; so much, in fact, that Umrik’s existence stopped being debated almost a hundred millennia before the UCC even came to be. Umrik left innumerable messages across the universe, inscribed in nearly every medium conceivable. Every message contains three things: A detailed account of how Umrik brought mass and matter into this world and shaped it to create life (the big bang), a lengthy poem (it is debated whether this counts as a poem; the grammatical subjects of each “lyric” correspond in a rhythmic scheme) dedicated to Umrik and his self-acclaimed greatness, and finally a single-sentence warning that just reads: “Do not heed the words of Ae-Khorim-Aul-Allaust.” It is entirely unknown who or what the warning refers to, though many have theories. The entire message is conveyed via a direct mental translation (along with an understanding of how to pronounce the names of entities mentioned) the moment the reader observes the inscription, which results in a slightly different meaning being translated from the message depending on the languages the translator knows. This used to cause no small amount of confusion amongst scholars. The script these messages are written in is indescribable and impossible to reproduce.
>>>

>>Ae-Khorim-Aul-Allaust
>>>
  Ae-Khorim-Aul-Allaust is a member of the same species and civilization as Umrik-Kaust’bringer. Much like Umrik brought Uithyl-Kaust to this world, Khorim brought Ae-Aukh into this world. Ae-Aukh is the metaphysical power, the fundament of condensing and transmuting all things Aukh through non-linear means, accessing a space with no dimensions or area to speak of. Ae-Aukh is a zero-dimensional omnipresent mutable database. In other words, Ae-Aukh is this world’s magic medium.
  Objects made of Seios-Aukh can be converted into Aephon, a shared medium that uses the shared Aukh nature as a platform to converge Seios and Ae. 
  Aephon is physical Seios-Aukh objects (space, gravity, light, etc.) made into a zero-dimensional replica of themselves that, if suddenly exposed to three dimensions once more, automatically returns the objects to their original forms.
>>>

>>The Shadow 
>>>
  The shadow is malevolent. It is hostile to both Seios-Aukh and Uithyl-Kaust.
  It is inherently destructive. It is antithetic to Uithyl-Kaust and negates the unrefined form of Aukh.
  It is also a fundament of Lunui. It should not be possible for something inherently destructive in such a manner to become as structurally foundational to the universe as a fundament. And yet, it is.
  It has existed for so long that it has dissolved its way into the Axiallia. It is considered a third, separate type of fundament by the universe itself. Uithyl is of Kaust, Seios is of Aukh, and the shadow is of neither.
  Though the fundament of shadow has existed longer than the named Uithyl-Kaust, it remains unnamed. It has remained nearly invisible for most of existence. Few civilizations have ever had any dealings with it, and those records have long been lost to time.
  Until now.
  Abavan is flooded with the shadow fundament. It's behaving exactly as previously posited. It cannot be destroyed, thanks to the fundament having made its way into the Axialla.
>>>

Abavan
>Backstory
>>>
  Abavan was created by the UCC as the home for an experiment on human life. Primitive humans were brought to the planet after it was populated with plant life, but that was all that was put on the planet. Humanity’s greatest threats on Abavan were themselves and toxic plants. The goal was to observe natural human behavior in an environment without predator or prey. This was, of course, a terribly inaccurate way to find that out, as the UCC never ended up approving more than the first test planet. This is not a point of failure for the UCC (as a whole), however, as the UCC official who approved it was quite horrifically corrupt.
  Most humans on the planet died in the oncoming flood of shadow, However, some humans were kept in asteroid-proof simulated environments, built to mitigate the risk of sudden extinction.
>>>

>Shadows Ever Malevolent
>>>
  The shadow came like a flood. The destruction was impossible to track in the chaos it birthed.
  The first shadow to arise was the muck. The fluid darkness bubbled out of the earth unpredictably. It dissolves any and all physical matter it touches, converting it into a formless shadowy substance not composed of atoms that acts like a non-newtonian fluid and retains its shape but no other properties (heat, momentum, friction, etc.).
  The second shadow to arise was the beasts. The monsters began formation from the oceans of shadow spreading across the planet. Their touch and bite share the dissolving effect of the shadowy muck, making them an extremely dangerous foe. They have the approximate consciousness and intelligence of wildcats. They can appear in a multitude of shapes, all of which are illogical and unrecognizable as any other existing lifeform.
>>>
>Ever Radiant
>>>Amongst the disaster the shadow flood brought, a flare of hope began to grow invisibly amongst the primitive humans that lived on the planet. The phenomenon spread during the flood without any need for reproduction or interspecies contact, which fortunately meant that it reached the humans in the protected environment-enclosures. When it was discovered much later, it was eventually given the name of “Ae'lines.”

>Ae’lines
>>>
  Ae’lines are Ae-Aukh wires made physical occupants of base reality. They have physical dimensions that can be measured, are effectively impenetrable, and technically contain nothing (speaking three-dimensionally) because their innards are Ae-Aukh. It remains unknown how the zero-dimensional Ae-Aukh is able to form three-dimensional Ae’lines (and how the inside of an Ae’line is still Ae-Aukh).
  They are designed to contain and transmit Aephon. Their endpoints (Ae’nodes) naturally produce a material called Phenaera in crystalline structures. Ae’nodes also continuously emit electrical pulses, causing the Phenaera to restructure itself for the purpose of gathering Seios-Aukh and converting it into Aephon. The Ae’lines themselves also respond to external electrical signals coming through their nodes (when the attached Phenaera cell is not electric-type). This phenomenon allows Ae’lines to be controlled in a manner similar to limbs or tentacles, potentially enabling the controller to move the Ae’line with extreme precision and speed.
  Ae’lines perpetually emit pure white light. This just happens. Nobody knows exactly why. The total amount emitted is not particularly high, though it does seem to increase when Aephon is flowing through the Ae’line. The light still shines even when no Aephon is present within, though, so it isn’t purely a case of leakage through radiation.
  The thickness of an Ae’line corresponds to the size of the attached Phenaera cell. On Abavan this is usually similar to the thickness of a strand of hair.
>>>

>Phenaera
>>>
  Phenaera is a material that grows on Ae’nodes. It is nonliving and forms cellular structures in response to the electrical pulses constantly emitted by the Ae’nodes. Phenaera cells are built to absorb the various energies of Seios-Aukh, but they physically cannot absorb more than one type at once. Thus, Phenaera cells must specialize and pick a type of Seios-Aukh to absorb. It is unknown what factors drive this decision. On Abavan, the type chosen by every Phenaera cell is Seios-Aukh-Ylluni, a.k.a light. Light-type Phenaera cells absorb extremely high percentages of light that hit them, making them appear almost completely black and unreflective. The absorbed light is delivered directly into the attached Ae’line, preventing the light from ever being converted into heat.
  Every human on Abavan has a small coating of Phenaera cells across the backside of their neck, creating an uncannily smooth black surface. Once an Ae’line has grown cells at one end, the other Ae’node will begin growing a Phenaera emitter. Each Ae’line will ultimately have one cell and one emitter. Once this state is achieved, Phenaera pauses growth on that Ae’line and the electrical pulses being emitted stop. The emitter is responsible for controlling the rate and direction of output Aephon as it exits the Ae’line. The emitter is NOT responsible for converting Aephon into Seios-Aukh; that happens automatically as Aephon exits an Ae’line.
  Abavan humans have their Ae’lines hanging freely, outside their bodies, each attached to a corresponding Phenaera cell on their neck. The Phenaera cells are attached to the nervous system. This, in tandem with the fact that the Ae’lines aren’t restrained by the body, allows the brain to control the attached Ae’lines freely. The Ae’lines natural luminescence combined with the movement makes them look like the user has gotten their unnaturally long hair possessed.
